CONTROL + C
------------------------------------------------------------

in most terminals, ctrl + c means:
“stop this process.”

here, ctrl + c is also a reminder:

you are allowed to stop.

you are allowed to interrupt patterns that are hurting you.
you are allowed to exit loops that are draining you.
you are allowed to say “no” to systems that don’t serve you.

in this shell, ctrl + c:
- cancels the current input
- clears the line
- and quietly reminds you:
  you are not trapped.
