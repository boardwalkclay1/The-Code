FLASH LEARNING — FULL BREAKDOWN

flash learning works because it bypasses the slow, padded, school‑style learning loop.

traditional learning:
- long sessions
- low retention
- low emotion
- high boredom
- high dropout

flash learning:
- short bursts
- high retention
- high emotion
- high pattern recognition
- low friction

why it works:
your brain is built to notice:
- contrast
- speed
- rhythm
- repetition
- emotional charge

flash learning uses all five.

diagram:
+------------------+
|  FLASH SEQUENCE  |
+------------------+
|  1. word         |
|  2. meaning      |
|  3. example      |
|  4. recall hit   |
+------------------+

each flash sequence is a micro‑lesson.
each micro‑lesson builds a pattern.
patterns become skills.

flash learning is used:
- before a course (priming)
- during a course (reinforcement)
- after a course (retention)
- inside games (pattern training)
- inside terminal 2 (mental sharpening)

flash learning is not passive.
