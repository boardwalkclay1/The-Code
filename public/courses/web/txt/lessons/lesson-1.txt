LESSON 1 — THE REAL BEGINNING

PART 1 — WHAT YOU’RE ACTUALLY LEARNING
You’re learning how the web works from the ground up.  
Not the fake version people pretend to understand.  
The real version that gives you control.

PART 2 — WHY THIS MATTERS
Everything you see online is built from the same simple pieces.  
Once you understand those pieces, nothing feels confusing anymore.  
You stop being a consumer and start being a builder.

PART 3 — THE THREE CORE LANGUAGES
Websites are built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.  
These three languages work together like a small team.  
Each one has a job, and none of them are complicated.

PART 4 — WHAT HTML REALLY IS
HTML is the structure of a page.  
It decides what exists: text, images, buttons, sections.  
It’s the skeleton, nothing more.

PART 5 — WHAT CSS REALLY IS
CSS controls how things look.  
Colors, spacing, fonts, layout — all of that is CSS.  
It’s the style layer that makes a page feel alive.

PART 6 — WHAT JAVASCRIPT REALLY IS
JavaScript controls behavior.  
It makes things react, move, update, and respond.  
It’s the logic layer that brings everything to life.

PART 7 — HOW THEY CONNECT
These three languages must be linked together to work.  
HTML loads CSS and JavaScript into the page.  
When they’re connected, the website becomes a complete system.

PART 8 — WHY THIS IS SIMPLE
You don’t need to memorize anything right now.  
You just need to understand what each part does.  
The details come later, naturally.

PART 9 — THE FIRST SKILL YOU’RE BUILDING
You’re learning how to think in layers.  
Structure, style, behavior — three layers, one page.  
This mindset makes everything easier later.

PART 10 — THE TERMINAL YOU WILL USE
You’ll use the Web Terminal to create files and folders.  
It works like a real developer terminal but without the stress.  
Every command you type teaches your brain how the system works.

PART 11 — WHY THE TERMINAL MATTERS
Developers use terminals every day.  
It’s faster, cleaner, and more powerful than clicking around.  
You’re learning the real workflow from day one.

PART 12 — PREVIEW MODE
Preview Mode shows you the website you’re building.  
It updates as you create files and write code.  
It’s the closest thing to watching your ideas appear in real time.

PART 13 — THE GITHUB COURSE YOU GOT
You also have the GitHub course included.  
GitHub teaches you how to save, track, and publish your work.  
It’s the backbone of real development.

PART 14 — THE BASH COURSE YOU GOT
You also have the Bash course included.  
Bash teaches you how to control your computer with commands.  
It makes you faster and more confident than most beginners.

PART 15 — WHY WE MOVE SLOW
Moving slow builds real understanding.  
You’re not rushing through information.  
You’re building a foundation that won’t collapse later.

PART 16 — WHAT YOU SHOULD FEEL
It’s normal to feel unsure at first.  
You’re learning a new language and a new way of thinking.  
But everything here is designed to make sense step by step.

PART 17 — WHAT YOU’RE NOT DOING
You’re not memorizing.  
You’re not guessing.  
You’re not pretending to understand things you don’t.

PART 18 — WHAT YOU ARE DOING
You’re learning the real rules of how the web works.  
You’re building confidence through clarity.  
You’re taking control of something most people never understand.

PART 19 — THE GOAL OF LESSON 1
By the end of this lesson, you should know what HTML, CSS, and JavaScript are.  
You should know how they connect.  
And you should know why the terminal and preview mode matter.

PART 20 — YOU’RE READY FOR WHAT’S NEXT
You now understand the three core languages and the tools you’ll use.  
The next lesson will show you how to create your first real structure.  
You’re moving forward with strength, not confusion.
