LESSON 1 — WELCOME, YOU’RE SAFE HERE

Hey… slow down. Breathe. You’re okay.

I know all these words like “HTML” and “JavaScript” might look big and scary right now, but you’re going to learn them one tiny step at a time. You don’t have to know anything yet. You’re just a baby taking your first steps, and I’m holding your hand the whole way.

Let’s start super simple.

HTML is just the words on the page.
CSS is just the colors and the style.
JavaScript is just the brain that makes things move.

That’s it. Three little friends.

They work together like this:
HTML says “here I am.”
CSS says “let me make you pretty.”
JavaScript says “let me make you smart.”

You don’t have to memorize anything. You’ll see it over and over until it feels easy.

And guess what? You also got the GitHub and Bash courses for free. Those will help you save your files, create folders, and publish your websites like a real developer. But don’t worry about that yet. One step at a time.

You’ll also get access to the Web Terminal. It looks like a real developer terminal, but it’s friendly. You can type commands like:

mkdir pages
touch index.html

And it will actually create the files in your virtual folder structure. Then you can switch to Preview Mode and see your website appear like magic.

You’re doing great already.
