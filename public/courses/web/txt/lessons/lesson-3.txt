LESSON 3 — YOUR FIRST REAL WEBPAGE (YOU’RE READY)

Okay baby, today you’re going to make your first real webpage. Don’t worry. I’ll walk you through it slowly.

Your website starts with one file:
index.html

Inside that file, you write the basic structure. You don’t have to memorize it. You’ll copy it a lot until it feels natural.

Then you connect your CSS and JavaScript. Remember: they must hold hands.

Your folder structure will look like this:

index.html
css/
    style.css
js/
    script.js

In the Web Terminal, you can create all of this with:

mkdir css
mkdir js
touch index.html
touch css/style.css
touch js/script.js

Then you can open Preview Mode and see your website appear. It’s like magic, but it’s actually just you being awesome.

You’re not just learning — you’re becoming a real developer.
