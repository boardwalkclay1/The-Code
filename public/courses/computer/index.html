1. What a computer is and what it does

A computer is a machine that takes input, processes it using instructions, and produces output. It stores data, runs programs, connects to networks, and automates tasks that would be slow or impossible by hand.

With a computer, you can create, learn, communicate, design, code, and manage information. Without one, you rely on slower, manual, or limited tools.

Advantages of having a computer

  • Faster access to information and learning
  • Ability to create documents, art, code, and media
  • Automation of repetitive tasks
  • Communication with people and systems worldwide
  • Better organization of files, notes, and projects

Without a computer

  • More manual work and slower workflows
  • Limited access to digital tools and opportunities
  • Harder to practice modern skills (coding, design, remote work)

2. Why math and spelling matter for computers

Computers are logical. Commands, file names, and code must be typed accurately. Practicing math sharpens your logic and problem‑solving. Practicing spelling improves your accuracy when typing commands, paths, and code.

It’s not just about typing fast. It’s about typing correctly and thinking clearly.

3. It’s not all about typing speed

Typing speed helps, but real power comes from shortcuts. Shortcuts reduce clicks, save time, and make you feel in control instead of lost.

You’ll learn shortcuts for:

  • PowerShell
  • Bash
  • Git/GitHub
  • Saving, copying, and managing files
