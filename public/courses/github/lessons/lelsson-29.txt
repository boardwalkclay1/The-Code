# GITHUB LESSON 29 — THE REPOSITORY TIMELINE

=== PART 1 — [Timeline](ca://s?q=Explain_repository_timeline) ===
The timeline shows every saved moment in your project.  
It lists all commits in order.  
It is the full history of your work.

=== PART 2 — [Commit List](ca://s?q=Commit_list_explained) ===
Each entry in the timeline is a commit.  
A commit represents a saved change.  
GitHub shows who made it and when.

=== PART 3 — [Commit Messages](ca://s?q=Commit_messages_explained) ===
Each commit includes a message.  
The message explains what changed.  
Clear messages make history easy to understand.

=== PART 4 — [Author Information](ca://s?q=Commit_author_info) ===
The timeline shows who made each commit.  
This helps track contributions.  
It is useful for teams.

=== PART 5 — [Timestamps](ca://s?q=Commit_timestamps) ===
Each commit includes a timestamp.  
This shows when the change happened.  
It helps you understand project progress.

=== PART 6 — [Change Details](ca://s?q=Commit_diff_explained) ===
You can click a commit to see what changed.  
GitHub shows added and removed lines.  
This makes reviewing history simple.

=== PART 7 — [Branch History](ca://s?q=Branch_history) ===
Each branch has its own timeline.  
This shows the work done on that branch.  
It keeps versions separate.

=== PART 8 — [Merge History](ca://s?q=Merge_history) ===
Merges appear in the timeline.  
They show when branches were combined.  
This helps track major updates.

=== PART 9 — [Revert Options](ca://s?q=Revert_commit) ===
You can revert a commit from the timeline.  
Reverting creates a new commit that undoes the change.  
This protects your project from mistakes.

=== PART 10 — [Compare View](ca://s?q=Compare_commits) ===
You can compare two commits.  
GitHub shows the differences between them.  
This helps you understand how the project evolved.

=== PART 11 — [Search History](ca://s?q=Search_commit_history) ===
You can search the timeline by keyword.  
This helps you find specific changes.  
It is useful for large projects.

=== PART 12 — [Contributor Tracking](ca://s?q=Timeline_contributors) ===
The timeline shows contributions from all team members.  
This helps track who did what.  
It improves accountability.

=== PART 13 — [Activity Overview](ca://s?q=Repository_activity) ===
The timeline gives a quick overview of project activity.  
You can see busy periods and slow periods.  
This helps with planning.

=== PART 14 — [Project Growth](ca://s?q=Project_growth_timeline) ===
The timeline shows how your project grows over time.  
Each commit adds to the story.  
This creates a clear development path.

=== PART 15 — [Mistake Recovery](ca://s?q=Timeline_mistake_recovery) ===
The timeline helps you recover from mistakes.  
You can return to earlier versions.  
This makes your work safer.

=== PART 16 — [Team Coordination](ca://s?q=Timeline_team_coordination) ===
Teams use the timeline to understand each other’s work.  
It shows what changed and why.  
This improves collaboration.

=== PART 17 — [Transparency](ca://s?q=Timeline_transparency) ===
The timeline makes all changes visible.  
Nothing is hidden.  
This builds trust.

=== PART 18 — [Learning Tool](ca://s?q=Timeline_learning_tool) ===
The timeline helps beginners learn how projects evolve.  
You can study past changes.  
It teaches good habits.

=== PART 19 — [Why Timeline Matters](ca://s?q=Why_timeline_matters) ===
The timeline is the backbone of your project history.  
It shows every step taken.  
It keeps your work organized.

Releases help you package important versions.  
They are useful for sharing finished work.
