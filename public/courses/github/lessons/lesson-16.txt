# GITHUB LESSON 16 — HOW TEAMS REVIEW EACH OTHER’S WORK (“CHECKING CHANGES”)
# 20 PARTS — ZERO KNOWLEDGE

=== PART 1 — WHAT IS REVIEWING? ===
reviewing means looking at someone’s work.
seeing what they did.
understanding their changes.

=== PART 2 — REVIEWING IN REAL LIFE ===
teachers review homework.
coaches review plays.
friends review ideas.

=== PART 3 — REVIEWING IN A PROJECT ===
you look at someone’s changes.
you understand their idea.
you give feedback.

=== PART 4 — WHY REVIEWING MATTERS ===
reviewing keeps quality high.
reviewing catches mistakes.
reviewing helps learning.

=== PART 5 — WHAT GIT DOES FOR REVIEWING ===
git shows differences.
git shows versions.
git shows changes.

=== PART 6 — WHAT GITHUB DOES FOR REVIEWING ===
github displays changes clearly.
github lets people comment.
github lets people approve.

=== PART 7 — WHAT A REVIEWER DOES ===
they read the changes.
they understand the idea.
they give suggestions.

=== PART 8 — WHAT A REVIEWER DOES NOT DO ===
they do not judge the person.
they do not attack the idea.
they help improve the work.

=== PART 9 — WHAT A GOOD REVIEW LOOKS LIKE ===
clear
kind
helpful

=== PART 10 — WHAT A BAD REVIEW LOOKS LIKE ===
confusing
mean
unclear

=== PART 11 — WHY REVIEWS HELP TEAMS ===
teams grow together.
teams learn together.
teams improve together.

=== PART 12 — WHAT YOU CONTROL ===
you choose how to review.
you choose how to respond.
you choose how to improve.

=== PART 13 — WHAT GIT CONTROLS ===
git controls:
the differences
the structure
the clarity

=== PART 14 — WHAT GITHUB CONTROLS ===
github controls:
the display
the comments
the approvals

=== PART 15 — WHY THIS IS POWERFUL ===
reviewing makes you better.
reviewing makes your team better.
reviewing makes your project better.

=== PART 16 — WHAT YOU KNOW NOW ===
you understand:
reviewing
feedback
improvement

you are learning how teams grow.
