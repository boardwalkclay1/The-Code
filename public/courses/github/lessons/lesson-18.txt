# GITHUB LESSON 18 — HOW YOUR WORK TRAVELS FROM YOUR COMPUTER TO THE INTERNET
# 20 PARTS — ZERO KNOWLEDGE

=== PART 1 — WHAT DOES “TRAVEL” MEAN? ===
travel means moving from one place to another.
your work can travel too.

=== PART 2 — TRAVEL IN REAL LIFE ===
a letter travels to a friend.
a package travels to a house.
a message travels to a phone.

=== PART 3 — TRAVEL IN COMPUTERS ===
your work can move from your computer to the internet.

=== PART 4 — WHY WORK TRAVELS ===
so you can:
back it up
share it
protect it
access it anywhere

=== PART 5 — WHAT IS “THE INTERNET”? ===
the internet is a giant network of computers.
they talk to each other.
they share information.

=== PART 6 — WHAT IS ONLINE STORAGE? ===
online storage is a place on the internet where your work lives.
it is safe.
it is backed up.
it is always available.

=== PART 7 — WHAT GITHUB DOES ===
github stores your work online.
github protects it.
github organizes it.

=== PART 8 — WHAT GIT DOES ===
git prepares your work.
git organizes your versions.
git gets your work ready to travel.

=== PART 9 — WHAT YOU DO ===
you decide when your work should travel.
you decide what should travel.

=== PART 10 — WHAT TRAVEL LOOKS LIKE ===
your work moves from:
your computer → github

=== PART 11 — WHY THIS IS POWERFUL ===
your work is safe.
your work is backed up.
your work is shareable.

=== PART 12 — WHAT HAPPENS BEFORE TRAVEL ===
you create.
you change.
you save moments.

=== PART 13 — WHAT HAPPENS DURING TRAVEL ===
git sends your moments.
github receives them.

=== PART 14 — WHAT HAPPENS AFTER TRAVEL ===
your work is online.
your work is protected.
your work is accessible.

=== PART 15 — WHY TEAMS NEED THIS ===
teams need one place to see everything.
github is that place.

=== PART 16 — WHAT YOU CONTROL ===
you choose when to send your work.
you choose what to send.

=== PART 17 — WHAT GIT CONTROLS ===
git controls:
the packaging
the structure
the order

=== PART 18 — WHAT GITHUB CONTROLS ===
github controls:
the storage
the safety
the sharing

=== PART 19 — WHAT YOU KNOW NOW ===
you understand:
travel

github’s role
