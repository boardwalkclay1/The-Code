# GITHUB LESSON 5 — HOW GIT ORGANIZES YOUR MOMENTS INTO A TIMELINE
# 20 PARTS — ZERO KNOWLEDGE

=== PART 1 — WHAT IS ORGANIZATION? ===
organization means putting things in order.
git puts your moments in order.

=== PART 2 — WHAT IS A TIMELINE? ===
a timeline is a line of moments.
moment 1
moment 2
moment 3
each one follows the last.

=== PART 3 — WHY ORDER MATTERS ===
order shows your journey.
order shows your progress.
order shows your growth.

=== PART 4 — WHAT GIT DOES WITH MOMENTS ===
git lines them up.
git numbers them.
git remembers them.

=== PART 5 — WHAT YOU SEE ===
you see a clean list.
you see your steps.
you see your story.

=== PART 6 — WHAT GIT SEES ===
git sees:
changes
differences
patterns

=== PART 7 — WHAT GITHUB SEES ===
github sees:
your timeline
your moments
your progress

=== PART 8 — WHY TIMELINES HELP YOU ===
timelines help you:
undo mistakes
compare versions
learn from your past

=== PART 9 — WHY TIMELINES HELP TEAMS ===
teams can see:
what happened
when it happened
why it happened

=== PART 10 — WHAT IS A “STEP” AGAIN? ===
a step is a moment.
a moment is a snapshot.
a snapshot is a memory.

=== PART 11 — WHAT IS A “CHANGE STORY”? ===
a change story is your timeline.
it shows how your project grew.

=== PART 12 — WHAT IS A “CLEAN TIMELINE”? ===
a clean timeline is:
organized
clear
easy to follow

=== PART 13 — WHAT MAKES A TIMELINE MESSY? ===
messy timelines happen when:
moments are random
moments are unclear
moments are too frequent

=== PART 14 — HOW TO KEEP IT CLEAN ===
create moments when:
you finish something
you fix something
you add something important

=== PART 15 — WHAT GIT DOES AUTOMATICALLY ===
git organizes.
git stores.
git protects.

=== PART 16 — WHAT YOU DO MANUALLY ===
you choose moments.
you choose clarity.
you choose your story.

=== PART 17 — WHY THIS MATTERS ===
a clean timeline makes you powerful.
you understand your work.
you control your work.

=== PART 18 — WHAT YOU KNOW NOW ===
you understand:
timelines
organization
clarity
