# GITHUB LESSON 1 — WHAT A “COMPUTER PROJECT” EVEN IS
# 20 PARTS — ZERO KNOWLEDGE REQUIRED

=== PART 1 — WHAT IS A COMPUTER PROJECT? ===
a computer project is something you build on a computer.
it could be a story.
it could be a picture.
it could be a website.
it could be anything you create.

=== PART 2 — WHAT IS A PIECE OF WORK? ===
a piece of work is something you make.
a drawing.
a paragraph.
a button on a screen.
anything you create is “work”.

=== PART 3 — WHAT IS A CHANGE? ===
a change is when you do something new.
you add something.
you remove something.
you fix something.
you adjust something.

=== PART 4 — WHY CHANGES MATTER ===
changes matter because they shape your project.
every little change builds the final thing.
small steps become big results.

=== PART 5 — WHAT IS KEEPING TRACK? ===
keeping track means remembering what you did.
what you added.
what you removed.
what you changed.
keeping track helps you understand your progress.

=== PART 6 — WHY HUMANS FORGET ===
humans forget what they did yesterday.
computers do not forget.
computers can remember every step if we teach them how.

=== PART 7 — WHAT IS A COMPUTER MEMORY SYSTEM? ===
a computer memory system is something that remembers your steps.
it remembers what you did.
it remembers when you did it.
it remembers how things looked before and after.

=== PART 8 — WHY WE NEED A MEMORY SYSTEM ===
without a memory system:
you lose track.
you get confused.
you can’t undo mistakes.
you can’t see your progress.

=== PART 9 — WHAT IS GIT? (NO JARGON) ===
git is a memory system for your work.
git remembers every step you take.
git remembers every change you make.
git remembers your entire journey.

=== PART 10 — WHAT DOES GIT DO FOR YOU? ===
git helps you:
go back in time
see what changed
see who changed it
try new ideas safely
fix mistakes without fear

=== PART 11 — WHAT IS A “STEP” IN GIT? ===
a step is a moment where you say:
“this is what my work looks like right now.”
git remembers that moment forever.

=== PART 12 — WHAT IS A “PROJECT AREA”? ===
a project area is the place where your work lives.
it is where your pieces of work sit.
it is where your changes happen.

=== PART 13 — WHAT IS A “SAVE MOMENT”? ===
a save moment is when you tell git:
“remember this version.”
git takes a memory picture of your work.

=== PART 14 — WHY SAVE MOMENTS MATTER ===
save moments let you:
undo mistakes
compare versions
learn from your steps
build with confidence

=== PART 15 — WHAT IS GITHUB? (NO JARGON) ===
github is a website that stores your git memories online.
it is like a giant notebook for your work.
it keeps your steps safe.
it lets you share your work with others.

=== PART 16 — WHY GITHUB EXISTS ===
github exists so:
your work is safe
your work is backed up
your work can be shared
your work can be seen anywhere

=== PART 17 — GIT + GITHUB TOGETHER ===
git remembers your steps.
github stores those steps online.
together they protect your work.

=== PART 18 — WHY THIS MATTERS FOR YOU ===
with git and github:
you never lose progress
you never lose ideas
you never lose steps
you never lose your work

=== PART 19 — WHAT YOU KNOW NOW ===
you now understand:
what work is
what changes are
why memory matters
what git is
what github is
