# GITHUB LESSON 4 — WHAT A “MOMENT” REALLY IS
# 20 PARTS — ZERO KNOWLEDGE

=== PART 1 — WHAT IS A MOMENT? ===
a moment is a freeze-frame of your work.
like pausing a movie.
you capture how everything looks right now.

=== PART 2 — WHY MOMENTS MATTER ===
moments let you:
go back
compare
recover
learn

=== PART 3 — WHAT IS A “SAVE MOMENT”? ===
a save moment is when you tell git:
“remember this version of my work.”

=== PART 4 — WHAT DOES GIT DO WITH THIS? ===
git takes a memory picture.
git stores it.
git adds it to your timeline.

=== PART 5 — WHAT IS A TIMELINE AGAIN? ===
a timeline is your project’s story.
each moment is a chapter.
each chapter is remembered.

=== PART 6 — WHAT HAPPENS BEFORE A MOMENT? ===
you work.
you create.
you change things.
git watches.

=== PART 7 — WHAT HAPPENS DURING A MOMENT? ===
you pause.
you say:
“this is important.”
git remembers it.

=== PART 8 — WHAT HAPPENS AFTER A MOMENT? ===
you keep working.
you keep creating.
you keep changing things.
git keeps watching.

=== PART 9 — WHY HUMANS NEED MOMENTS ===
humans forget.
computers do not.
moments protect your progress.

=== PART 10 — WHAT IF YOU BREAK SOMETHING? ===
you can go back to a moment.
you can restore your work.
you can undo mistakes.

=== PART 11 — WHAT IF YOU TRY SOMETHING NEW? ===
you can create a moment first.
then experiment safely.
you can always return.

=== PART 12 — WHAT IF YOU WANT TO SHARE? ===
moments help others understand your journey.
moments show your steps.
moments show your growth.

=== PART 13 — WHAT IS A GOOD MOMENT? ===
a good moment is:
clear
important
meaningful

=== PART 14 — WHAT IS A BAD MOMENT? ===
a bad moment is:
confusing
random
unclear

=== PART 15 — WHY MOMENTS SHOULD BE CLEAR ===
clear moments help you later.
clear moments help others.
clear moments help your future self.

=== PART 16 — WHAT YOU CONTROL ===
you choose when to create moments.
you choose what matters.
you choose your timeline.

=== PART 17 — WHAT GIT CONTROLS ===
git controls:
the memory
the storage
the organization

=== PART 18 — WHAT GITHUB CONTROLS ===
github controls:
the online backup
the sharing
the safety

=== PART 19 — WHAT YOU KNOW NOW ===
you understand:
what a moment is
why moments matter
how moments protect you
