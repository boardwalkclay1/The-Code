# GITHUB LESSON 17 — WHAT A “REQUEST TO REVIEW” IS (THE IDEA BEHIND PULL REQUESTS)
# 20 PARTS — ZERO KNOWLEDGE

=== PART 1 — WHAT IS A REQUEST? ===
a request is asking for something.
asking for help.
asking for attention.

=== PART 2 — REQUESTS IN REAL LIFE ===
“can you look at this?”
“can you check this?”
“can you help me?”

=== PART 3 — WHAT IS A REVIEW REQUEST? ===
a review request is asking someone to check your work.

=== PART 4 — WHY REVIEW REQUESTS MATTER ===
they bring attention.
they bring feedback.
they bring improvement.

=== PART 5 — WHAT GIT DOES WITH REQUESTS ===
git prepares your changes.
git organizes your branch.
git gets ready for review.

=== PART 6 — WHAT GITHUB DOES WITH REQUESTS ===
github shows your changes.
github lets people comment.
github lets people approve.

=== PART 7 — WHAT YOU DO ===
you finish your idea.
you prepare your branch.
you ask for a review.

=== PART 8 — WHAT THE TEAM DOES ===
they read your changes.
they understand your idea.
they give feedback.

=== PART 9 — WHAT HAPPENS AFTER FEEDBACK ===
you improve your work.
you adjust your idea.
you get ready again.

=== PART 10 — WHAT HAPPENS WHEN EVERYONE AGREES ===
your idea is ready.
your branch can join the main branch.
your work becomes part of the project.

=== PART 11 — WHY THIS IS POWERFUL ===
you grow.
your team grows.
your project grows.

=== PART 12 — WHAT YOU CONTROL ===
you choose when to request.
you choose who to ask.
you choose how to respond.

=== PART 13 — WHAT GIT CONTROLS ===
git controls:
the structure
the differences
the clarity

=== PART 14 — WHAT GITHUB CONTROLS ===
github controls:
the request
the comments
the approval

=== PART 15 — WHAT YOU KNOW NOW ===
you understand:
requests
reviews
teamwork

=== PART 16 — YOU ARE BUILDING SKILL ===
you are learning how teams collaborate.

=== PART 17 — YOU ARE GETTING READY ===

then the commands make sense.
