# GITHUB LESSON 10 — WHAT IT MEANS TO “SAVE” A MOMENT IN GIT
# 20 PARTS — ZERO KNOWLEDGE

=== PART 1 — WHAT IS SAVING? ===
saving means keeping something safe.
saving means not losing it.
saving means protecting it.

=== PART 2 — SAVING IN REAL LIFE ===
you save money.
you save photos.
you save memories.

=== PART 3 — SAVING ON A COMPUTER ===
saving means keeping your work.
saving means not losing progress.

=== PART 4 — WHAT GIT SAVES ===
git saves your grouped changes.
git saves your moments.
git saves your story.

=== PART 5 — WHAT A SAVE MOMENT IS ===
a save moment is when you tell git:
“remember this group of changes.”

=== PART 6 — WHY SAVE MOMENTS MATTER ===
save moments protect your progress.
save moments protect your ideas.
save moments protect your journey.

=== PART 7 — WHAT HAPPENS BEFORE A SAVE ===
you work.
you create.
you change things.

=== PART 8 — WHAT HAPPENS DURING A SAVE ===
you pause.
you say:
“this matters.”
git remembers it.

=== PART 9 — WHAT HAPPENS AFTER A SAVE ===
you keep working.
you keep creating.
you keep changing things.

=== PART 10 — WHY SAVING IS POWERFUL ===
saving gives you:
freedom
safety
confidence

=== PART 11 — WHAT GIT DOES WITH SAVES ===
git stores them.
git organizes them.
git protects them.

=== PART 12 — WHAT GITHUB DOES WITH SAVES ===
github stores them online.
github keeps them safe.
github lets you share them.

=== PART 13 — WHAT YOU CONTROL ===
you choose when to save.
you choose what matters.

=== PART 14 — WHAT GIT CONTROLS ===
git controls:
the memory
the timeline
the structure

=== PART 15 — WHAT GITHUB CONTROLS ===
github controls:
the online backup
the sharing
the safety

=== PART 16 — WHY SAVING IS A SKILL ===
saving at the right time helps you.
saving too often creates noise.
saving too rarely creates confusion.
