# GITHUB LESSON 14 — HOW TWO PATHS COME TOGETHER (“MERGING”)
# 20 PARTS — ZERO KNOWLEDGE

=== PART 1 — WHAT IS COMING TOGETHER? ===
coming together means joining.
two things become one.

=== PART 2 — COMING TOGETHER IN REAL LIFE ===
two ideas combine.
two roads meet.
two teams join.

=== PART 3 — WHAT IS COMING TOGETHER IN WORK? ===
your main path meets your experiment path.
your ideas combine.

=== PART 4 — THE REAL WORD FOR THIS ===
the real word is “merging”.
merging means joining two branches.

=== PART 5 — WHY MERGING MATTERS ===
merging lets you bring good ideas back.
merging lets you combine progress.
merging lets you grow your project.

=== PART 6 — WHAT HAPPENS BEFORE MERGING ===
you create a branch.
you explore.
you build something new.

=== PART 7 — WHAT HAPPENS DURING MERGING ===
git compares the paths.
git joins them together.
git creates one clean path.

=== PART 8 — WHAT HAPPENS AFTER MERGING ===
your main path now includes the new idea.
your project grows.
your work improves.

=== PART 9 — WHY MERGING IS SAFE ===
branches stay separate until you merge.
you choose when merging happens.
you stay in control.

=== PART 10 — WHAT GIT DOES DURING MERGING ===
git looks at differences.
git connects the paths.
git protects your history.

=== PART 11 — WHAT GITHUB DOES DURING MERGING ===
github shows the changes.
github helps teams review.
github helps teams approve.

=== PART 12 — WHAT YOU CONTROL ===
you choose when to merge.
you choose what to merge.
you choose how to merge.

=== PART 13 — WHAT GIT CONTROLS ===
git controls:
the joining
the structure
the safety

=== PART 14 — WHAT GITHUB CONTROLS ===
github controls:
the review
the visibility
the protection

=== PART 15 — WHY MERGING IS POWERFUL ===
merging lets you grow safely.

merging lets you build confidently.

=== PART 16 — WHAT YOU KNOW NOW ===
